ERP User Acceptance Testing Checklist

Use this checklist to organize business-led ERP testing. Tailor the scenarios and acceptance thresholds to your approved solution design.

Prepare

Test owner, business participants, and process approvers are assigned.

Test environment, access roles, configuration, and test data are ready.

Scenarios, expected results, prerequisites, and evidence requirements are documented.

Defect severity, triage cadence, retest, and sign-off rules are agreed.

Run end-to-end scenarios

Finance: journals, approvals, period close, and reconciliations.

Procurement: request, purchase order, receipt, invoice, and payment.

Sales: order entry, fulfillment, invoicing, and customer reporting.

Inventory and operations: receipts, transfers, adjustments, and production scenarios where applicable.

Integrations, reports, security roles, and exception handling.

Close and sign off

Results and evidence are recorded against each test scenario.

Critical defects are resolved or have an approved mitigation and owner.

Business owners confirm acceptance criteria and approve the release.

Open items, support responsibilities, and training needs are handed over.

This checklist is a starting point. Add scenarios for your organization's configured processes, controls, integrations, and reporting.
