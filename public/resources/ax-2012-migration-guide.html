AX 2012 to Dynamics 365 Migration Guide

A phased planning guide for organizations assessing a move from Microsoft Dynamics AX 2012 to Dynamics 365 Finance and Supply Chain Management.

1. Discover the current environment

  • Record AX version, modules, legal entities, locations, environments, and operational owners.
  • Inventory customizations, reports, batch jobs, integrations, ISV solutions, and infrastructure dependencies.
  • Map critical end-to-end processes and note where workarounds or manual controls exist.

2. Define the target design

  • Confirm business goals, release scope, process owners, and success measures.
  • Classify each customization: retire, use standard functionality, configure, or rebuild.
  • Document security roles, reporting requirements, integrations, and target operating processes.

3. Prepare and migrate data

  • Define master and transactional data scope, history requirements, retention, and ownership.
  • Profile source data; resolve duplicates and quality issues with accountable business owners.
  • Create source-to-target mappings and rehearse extraction, transformation, and loading with representative data.
  • Reconcile balances, open transactions, inventory, and other agreed control totals after each rehearsal.

4. Test business scenarios

  • Validate finance close, purchasing, sales, inventory, production, reporting, and integration scenarios.
  • Include security, exception handling, performance, and recovery tests where appropriate.
  • Use business-owned acceptance criteria and record sign-offs and unresolved issues.

5. Plan cutover and support

  • Assign owners and timing for data freeze, final migration, integrations, access, and go-live checks.
  • Agree go/no-go criteria, escalation paths, and contingency actions before the cutover rehearsal.
  • Prepare role-based training, support coverage, issue triage, and post-go-live stabilization.

Important: Treat the migration as a business transformation, not just a technical upgrade. Validate current product lifecycle, licensing, and platform guidance with Microsoft and your implementation partner.
