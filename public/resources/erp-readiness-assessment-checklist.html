ERP Readiness Assessment Checklist

Use this checklist with business owners and project stakeholders to identify decisions and risks before selecting or implementing an ERP platform. Mark each item Ready, In progress, or Needs attention and assign an owner.

Business goals and scope

Business outcomes and measures of success are documented.

In-scope companies, locations, departments, and processes are agreed.

Executive sponsor, process owners, and decision makers are identified.

First-release priorities and out-of-scope items are recorded.

Processes and people

Current finance, purchasing, sales, inventory, and operations workflows are mapped.

Process variations, approvals, and controls are understood.

Users for design reviews, testing, training, and sign-off are available.

Change impacts, communications, and role-based training needs are planned.

Data and integrations

Source systems, data owners, and retention requirements are listed.

Data quality issues, duplicates, and cleanup responsibilities are identified.

Required historical data and migration acceptance criteria are defined.

Integrations, reports, and external dependencies have owners and priorities.

Technology, security, and delivery

Identity, access roles, segregation of duties, and security reviews are planned.

Reporting, environments, deployment, and support responsibilities are understood.

Testing approach, issue triage, cutover, and contingency plans are documented.

Budget assumptions, licensing, implementation effort, and ongoing support are considered.

Open questions and owners

Record the highest-priority readiness gaps, the person responsible, and a target date.

This checklist is a planning aid, not a substitute for a scoped discovery and security assessment.
